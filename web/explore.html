Real parcels, real public data — running entirely in your browser. Pick a lane:

🔎
I'm scouting for opportunity Click any property to see what it is, what it's worth, whether it's for sale, the developer red-flags (slope, MUD, flood), and what it's best suited for. No use case needed.
🎯
I have a use in mind Pick a use — data center, warehouse club, fast-casual, housing — then click parcels to test the fit: what it needs nearby, what it induces, and whether it should be built.

Switch lanes anytime up top. The model is open YAML you can edit — change a threshold and this changes.

Compare pinned parcels

Pin a parcel from its analysis (📌 in the toolbar) to line it up against others here — size, value, and (in Test a use) the verdict.

🔒 Saved to local storage, never uploaded automatically. "Share list" puts these pins in a URL only when you copy it.

Bring your own data (private)

Drop pins from your own list — comps, sites you're tracking, parcels you own. One per line as lat, lng, label, or paste GeoJSON points.

🔒 Stays in your browser (saved to local storage). Never uploaded, never leaves your device.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Esc close the open dialog (this one, Compare, or your data)
